## How I Approach Platform Work

<div class="home-principles-grid">
  <div>
    <strong>Start with constraints.</strong>
    <p>Data sensitivity, compliance, failure modes, ownership, and support expectations shape the architecture before tools are selected.</p>
  </div>
  <div>
    <strong>Design for operators.</strong>
    <p>A platform should be understandable under pressure: observable, recoverable, documented, and safe to change.</p>
  </div>
  <div>
    <strong>Automate for evidence.</strong>
    <p>Good automation creates reviewed changes, repeatable delivery, visible drift, and recovery paths that teams can trust.</p>
  </div>
</div>
