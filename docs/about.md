## How I Think About Architecture

I start with the operating model before implementation details. The important
questions are usually not "which tool should we use?" but:

- What are the data boundaries?
- Who can make changes, and how are those changes reviewed?
- What does failure look like?
- What should be automated, and what should remain explicit?
- What evidence do we need for compliance, recovery, and debugging?
- Which parts of the platform must be standardised so teams can move faster?

This is why my portfolio leads with research infrastructure, security,
reliability, and platform architecture rather than a long technology list. The
tools matter, but they are only useful when they support a clear operating model.
